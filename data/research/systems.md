## Twine / Twining (트와인)

- **What:** Signature mechanic — after bonding with a captured Aniimo, the Pathfinder can **become** that Aniimo: see through its eyes and use its traversal and combat abilities directly (`confirmed` — PS/Xbox/Steam copy, FunPlus, Korean press).
- **Modes (community framing):** Command mode (direct party) vs Twine mode (player pilots the creature) (`community` — aniimoguide beginner guide).
- **Uses:** Glide / fly, swim, burrow, burn obstacles, puzzle elemental interactions, combat ultimates (`marketing` + Korean preview articles: ThisIsGame, IGN KR, Inven).
- **Twine-related growth:** Bonding required before Twine; exact friendship/bond meters and numeric thresholds **unknown** for sim (do not invent).

## Capture — Aniipod / 애니팟

- Field capture with Aniipods; timing, approach (rear), status effects, HP, and pod tier matter (`confirmed` — official odds page).
- Official KO capture formula page documents multipliers (`confirmed`):
  - Pods: 애니팟 ×1; 슈퍼 ×1.5; 스피드/트래킹/얼티밋 ×2; **챔피언 / 스파클 반드시 성공**; 빅화이트볼 ×2 (mass-spawn control ×6).
  - Out-of-combat / in-combat / fainted formulas differ (HP coeff in combat only; rear ×1.5 out of combat with normal pods).
  - Two-roll sqrt check for single-target normal pods.
- Marketing also mentions traps and environmental play (`marketing`).
- **Legendary / special capture:** Community notes launch rebuild around crafted Aniipod for legendaries; treat as `community` until official letter cited in-game.

Source: https://www.aniimo.com/ko/formula-multipliers

## Combat

- Real-time action combat; party roles (`community` synthesis of official role labels in Aniilog): **DPS, Break (무력화/격파), Support, Heal, Regen**.
- Nine elements: Fire, Water, Grass, Lightning, Earth, Wind, Dark, Ice, Light (`confirmed` via community catalogs mirroring official index; store copy says elemental interactions).
- Favourable/unfavourable multipliers reported as **1.6× / 0.625×** (`community` — aniimoguide; verify against in-game chart for wiki).
- Stagger / Break windows are central to squad building (`community`).
- **Do not invent DPS formulas or absolute damage numbers for the training sim.**

## Evolution (진화)

- Stages named **Lumin → Gamma → Nova** in EN community/official-adjacent data (`community` — widely used; confirm UI strings in live client).
- Not every line has three stages; some Lumin→Nova; some Nova-only in current data (`community`).
- **Branching evolution** by material / conditions; documented example: Pebbling → Earth Geodeback line **or** Fire Lavazar line (`community`).
- Regional / weather evolution forms change element and habitat (Highland, Beach, Rainstorm, etc.) (`community` + official Korean index form tabs).
- Korean press: most Aniimo support evolution (`marketing`).

## Camping RV / Homeland (캠핑카 · 홈랜드)

- Each Pathfinder owns a **personal RV** parked at open-world campsites; social meetups with other players (`confirmed` — Steam/Xbox/PS description).
- Returning to **Homeland** with the RV: plant crops, build decorations, arrange Aniimo, crafting / resource generation (`confirmed` store copy; Korean previews expand housing).
- **Hatchinator:** egg incubation with real-time timers; Caress can complete timers (observed ~20 uses; refresh cadence `unknown`) (`community`).
- Exact buildable item lists, plot sizes, multiplayer visit rules: `unknown` / partial community.
